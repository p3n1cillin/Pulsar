# Third-party notices

The airsoft scope firmware builds on the following third-party components.

## ESP-IDF

Espressif IoT Development Framework — RTOS, drivers, and the `esp_lcd` display
component used by the ST7789V driver. Licensed under the Apache License 2.0.

- https://github.com/espressif/esp-idf
- https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0

## esp32-camera

Espressif camera driver for OV2640/OV3660 (and other) DVP sensors, pulled in as an
IDF managed component. Licensed under the Apache License 2.0.

- https://github.com/espressif/esp32-camera

## esp_jpeg

JPEG codec component, installed as a dependency of esp32-camera. Licensed under the
Apache License 2.0.

- https://components.espressif.com/components/espressif/esp_jpeg
